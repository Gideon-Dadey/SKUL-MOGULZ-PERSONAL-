# Design notes

SKUL MOGULZ should look like a tool people use every day, not a pitch deck.

## Goals

- Make student work easy to scan
- Keep investor and mentor flows familiar without copying anyone beat for beat
- Stay light on decoration so content can breathe

## Visual direction

- Mint green accent (`#A5D6B7`)
- Clean white / soft gray surfaces
- Plus Jakarta Sans for UI text
- Soft corners, thin borders, restrained shadows

## Core screens

- **Login / signup** — simple form, clear next step
- **Feed** — updates first, filters second
- **Profile** — who someone is and what they are building
- **Network** — invitations and people nearby in the graph
- **Projects** — browse by stage and category

Screenshots of the current UI are in `docs/screenshots`.
