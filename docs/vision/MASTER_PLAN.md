# Product direction

SKUL MOGULZ started from a simple frustration: student founders do real work, but the tools around them are scattered. LinkedIn feels too polished for unfinished builds. Group chats disappear. Pitch decks sit unread.

The point of this product is to give builders a home base while the company is still messy.

## What we care about

1. **Creation over performance**  
   Profiles and posts should show what someone is shipping, not just how they brand themselves.

2. **Useful connections**  
   Matching should feel intentional. Mentors, co-founders, and early believers should be able to find each other without spam.

3. **Speed**  
   The app has to feel quick. If opening a chat or posting an update feels heavy, people bounce back to WhatsApp.

4. **Trust**  
   Investors and operators will only stick around if the product feels serious. Clean design and reliable messaging matter as much as features.

## Near-term focus

- Keep the feed honest and useful
- Make profiles a real portfolio of work
- Tighten messaging and notifications
- Grow campus by campus instead of chasing everyone at once

## Longer bets

- Better discovery for co-founders and collaborators
- Lightweight project launch surfaces
- Stronger analytics for founders who want to track traction
- Safer rooms for sensitive investor conversations

None of that matters if the basics are weak. First we make the daily loop solid: post, connect, message, come back tomorrow.
