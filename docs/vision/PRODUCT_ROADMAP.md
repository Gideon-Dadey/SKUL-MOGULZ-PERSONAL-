# Roadmap

This is a working list, not a promise. Order can change based on what users actually need.

## Done

- Auth and roles
- Feed and media posts
- Profiles and basic networking
- Messaging and calls
- Projects listing
- Mobile companion app

## Next

- Stronger project pages (clear stage, team, traction)
- Better notification center
- Search that works across people, posts, and projects
- Profile media and richer activity history

## Later

- Founder matchmaking with better filters
- Launch / waitlist surfaces for new products
- Optional funding and support flows
- Campus leaderboards and referral loops

## How we decide what ships

If a feature does not help a student founder show progress, find help, or stay in touch with people who matter, it waits.
